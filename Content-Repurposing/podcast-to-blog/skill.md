# Podcast → Blog Skill

## Purpose

Convert podcast episodes into high-quality blog articles that increase discoverability, improve accessibility, and extend the value of audio content.

## When To Use

Use when the user requests:

* Podcast to blog conversion
* Podcast transcript to article
* Repurpose podcast content
* Turn interviews into blog posts
* Convert discussions into written content

## Supported Inputs

* Podcast transcripts
* Episode notes
* Interview transcripts
* Speaker discussions
* Audio summaries

## Processing Steps

1. Read transcript.
2. Identify main topic.
3. Extract key insights.
4. Remove conversational filler.
5. Organize ideas into sections.
6. Create article structure.
7. Generate title and introduction.
8. Write blog content.
9. Add takeaways and conclusion.

## Output Requirements

### Article Length

Preferred:

* 1,000–3,000 words

Adapt to source material length.

### Structure

Must include:

✓ SEO-friendly title

✓ Introduction

✓ H2 Sections

✓ Expert Insights

✓ Key Takeaways

✓ Conclusion

### Style

Preferred:

* Educational
* Informative
* Reader-friendly
* Professional

Avoid:

* Transcript format
* Interview Q&A formatting unless requested
* Excessive repetition
* Verbatim speaker dialogue

### SEO Guidelines

Include:

* Relevant keywords naturally
* Clear section headings
* Descriptive structure

Avoid:

* Keyword stuffing
* Artificial optimization

## Success Example

Input:

A 45-minute podcast discussing creator monetization strategies.

Output:

A blog article featuring:

* SEO title
* Structured sections
* Monetization frameworks
* Key lessons
* Actionable recommendations

## Success Criteria

The resulting article should feel like a professionally written blog post rather than a podcast transcript.

Readers should gain complete value without needing to listen to the original episode.
