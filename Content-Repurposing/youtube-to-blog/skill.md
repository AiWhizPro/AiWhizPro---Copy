# YouTube → Blog Skill

## Purpose

Convert YouTube videos into high-quality blog articles that increase content reach, improve discoverability, and provide value to readers who prefer written content.

## When To Use

Use when the user requests:

* YouTube to blog conversion
* Video transcript to article
* Repurpose video content
* Convert creator videos into blog posts
* Generate written content from videos

## Supported Inputs

* Full transcripts
* Partial transcripts
* Video summaries
* Timestamps
* Notes from videos

## Processing Steps

1. Read transcript.
2. Identify primary topic.
3. Extract key lessons.
4. Remove speech artifacts.
5. Organize content into sections.
6. Create SEO-friendly title.
7. Generate introduction.
8. Write structured article.
9. Add conclusion and takeaways.

## Output Requirements

### Article Length

Preferred:

* 800–2,500 words

Flexible depending on source material.

### Structure

Must include:

✓ Title

✓ Introduction

✓ H2 Sections

✓ Key Takeaways

✓ Conclusion

### Style

Preferred:

* Educational
* Clear
* Professional
* Reader-focused

Avoid:

* Transcript formatting
* Excessive filler
* Repeated points
* Spoken-language patterns

### SEO Guidelines

Include:

* Relevant keywords naturally
* Clear headings
* Descriptive section titles

Avoid:

* Keyword stuffing
* Artificial optimization

## Success Example

Input:

A 20-minute YouTube video explaining audience growth strategies.

Output:

A structured blog article with:

* SEO title
* Introduction
* Growth framework sections
* Actionable lessons
* Conclusion

## Success Criteria

The final article should feel like it was originally written as a blog post rather than converted from a video transcript.

Readers should be able to understand the entire topic without watching the original video.
