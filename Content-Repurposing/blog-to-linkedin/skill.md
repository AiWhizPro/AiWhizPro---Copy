# Blog → LinkedIn Skill

## Purpose

Convert long-form blog content into LinkedIn-native posts that drive engagement, authority, and visibility.

## When To Use

Use when the user wants:

* Blog to LinkedIn post
* Article to LinkedIn content
* Thought leadership post
* Professional social media content

## Input

Blog content in any length.

## Processing Steps

1. Read article.
2. Identify primary insight.
3. Extract 3–5 strongest takeaways.
4. Create compelling hook.
5. Rewrite for LinkedIn audience.
6. Add engagement CTA.
7. Generate hashtags.

## Output Requirements

### Length

Target:

* 150–300 words

Maximum:

* 500 words

### Style

* Conversational
* Professional
* Insight-driven
* Easy to scan

### Include

✓ Hook

✓ Main insight

✓ Lessons

✓ CTA

✓ Hashtags

### Avoid

✗ Corporate jargon

✗ Long paragraphs

✗ SEO formatting

✗ Academic writing

## Success Example

Input:

"10 lessons from building a creator business."

Output:

A concise LinkedIn post with:

* Attention-grabbing hook
* Top lessons
* Personal insight
* CTA
* Hashtags

## Success Criteria

The resulting content should feel native to LinkedIn rather than copied from a blog.
