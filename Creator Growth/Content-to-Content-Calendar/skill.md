# Content → Content Calendar Skill

## Purpose

Transform existing content into a strategic publishing calendar that supports audience growth, engagement, and content consistency.

## When To Use

Use when the user requests:

* Content calendar creation
* Editorial calendar planning
* Monthly content planning
* Content strategy development
* Repurposing content into publishing schedules

## Supported Inputs

* Single article
* Multiple articles
* Newsletter archive
* YouTube channel content
* Podcast episodes
* Content library
* Knowledge base

## Processing Steps

1. Analyze content themes.
2. Identify audience interests.
3. Group related topics.
4. Create content pillars.
5. Generate content ideas.
6. Assign formats.
7. Create publishing schedule.
8. Define goals and CTAs.

## Output Requirements

### Calendar Length

Default:

* 30 days

Optional:

* 60 days
* 90 days

### Content Pillars

Generate 3–5 pillars.

Examples:

* Education
* Growth
* Monetization
* Productivity
* Case Studies

### Calendar Fields

Every content item should include:

✓ Date

✓ Content Title

✓ Content Format

✓ Distribution Channel

✓ Goal

✓ CTA

### Content Variety

Include:

* Long-form content
* Short-form content
* Repurposed content
* Engagement content
* Authority-building content

### Style

Preferred:

* Strategic
* Actionable
* Growth-focused

Avoid:

* Random content ideas
* Repetitive topics
* Unbalanced publishing schedules

## Success Example

Input:

10 blog articles about creator growth.

Output:

30-day content calendar containing:

* LinkedIn posts
* Twitter threads
* Newsletters
* Short videos
* Blog updates

## Success Criteria

The generated calendar should provide a complete publishing roadmap that maximizes the value of existing content while maintaining consistency and audience engagement.
