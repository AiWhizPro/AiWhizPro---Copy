# Content → Lead Magnet Skill

## Purpose

Transform existing content into downloadable lead magnets that increase email subscribers, lead generation, audience growth, and conversion opportunities.

## When To Use

Use when the user requests:

* Lead magnet creation
* Email subscriber growth assets
* Content upgrades
* Downloadable resources
* Opt-in incentives
* Conversion-focused content

## Supported Inputs

* Blog posts
* Articles
* Newsletters
* Videos
* Podcasts
* Educational content
* Courses
* Content collections

## Processing Steps

1. Analyze source content.
2. Identify valuable outcomes.
3. Extract actionable assets.
4. Select lead magnet format.
5. Create structure.
6. Write positioning.
7. Generate CTA recommendations.
8. Produce lead magnet outline.

## Output Requirements

### Generate

✓ Lead Magnet Title

✓ Promise Statement

✓ Target Audience

✓ Recommended Format

✓ Deliverable Outline

✓ CTA Recommendations

### Recommended Lead Magnet Formats

Choose the best fit:

* Checklist
* Cheatsheet
* Workbook
* Template
* Toolkit
* Resource Guide
* Swipe File
* Worksheet

### Value Requirements

The lead magnet should:

* Solve a specific problem
* Deliver a quick win
* Be immediately actionable
* Be easy to consume

### Positioning Requirements

Clearly communicate:

* Desired outcome
* Time saved
* Common mistakes avoided
* Benefits achieved

### CTA Requirements

Include:

* Download CTA
* Email opt-in CTA
* Follow-up nurture suggestions

## Success Example

Input:

A blog article about LinkedIn growth.

Output:

Lead Magnet:

"LinkedIn Growth Checklist"

Containing:

* Profile optimization checklist
* Posting framework
* Engagement checklist
* Weekly growth system

## Success Criteria

The resulting lead magnet should feel valuable enough that a user would willingly exchange their email address to access it.

The asset should create a clear connection between the content and a future product, service, or offer.
